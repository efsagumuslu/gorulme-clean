Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock other themes?
Collect stars by completing levels and daily quests, or watch a short ad to earn stars faster. Each theme unlocks for 100 stars.

How do power-ups work?
Power-ups (Invisibility and Speed Boost) unlock once you reach level 21. Use stars or watch a rewarded ad to activate them.

My lives ran out — what now?
Lives regenerate automatically over time, or you can watch a rewarded ad for an instant extra life.

The app isn't working correctly.
Please email us at the address above with your device model and a short description of the issue, and we'll look into it.
